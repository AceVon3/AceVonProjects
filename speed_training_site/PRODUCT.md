## Users

- **Primary buyer: State Farm agents** (agency owners). They decide for the whole office and pay for one account per agency. Scene: running a State Farm agency, watching lapses and cancellations, onboarding team members, wanting the office to sound professional without writing every email themselves. Range confirmed on the page: brand-new TICAs through Chairman's Circle, Trophy Club, MOA, MDRT, President's Club agents.
- **Daily users: team members** (office managers, customer service representatives, agent aspirants). They are the ones who send the emails and texts. The agent decides; the team uses.

## Product Purpose

SPEED Training gives a State Farm agency a done-for-you communication system: an On Demand workshop plus SPEED Cloud, which holds the email templates, text scripts, follow-up plans, signatures, and supporting tools the team uses every day. Success = the team communicates faster, more consistently, and more professionally from day one; processes survive when people come and go; new hires get up to speed in days, not months.

The site's job (Persuade): a first-time agent understands what SPEED is, believes it works for offices like theirs, and clicks through to Plans & Pricing. Confirmed primary action: **View Plans & Pricing** (Kartra page id `IhSs6KXnjgYf`).

## Positioning

Neighbors sell training or generic templates; SPEED sells the whole operating system for a State Farm agency's customer communication, written for State Farm workflows (ECRM follow-up plans, SFConnect texts, SMPs, BOD), used the same day an agency enrolls, and kept current in the cloud as compliance and tools change. ## Product Principles

1. Show the system, not the seminar. The templates, scripts, and follow-up plans are the product; the page should demonstrate what the team gets, not describe training in the abstract.
2. Speak agent to agent. Sean is a State Farm agent selling to State Farm agents; use their vocabulary (ECRM, SFConnect, TICA, Chairman's Circle) without explaining it away.
3. Same-day usefulness is the promise. Every section should reinforce "start running a system today," and the path to Plans & Pricing must be visible from every viewport.
4. Numbers and quotes are the proof. 4,000+, 125+, 60+, 10+ years, and the two testimonials carry belief; never pad them with invented claims.
5. Stay compliant and independent. Non-State Farm Vendor status and the disclaimer are part of the product's trustworthiness, not fine print to hide.
